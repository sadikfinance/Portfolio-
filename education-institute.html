01 / CERTIFICATION & RECOGNITION

Understand the certificate before enrolling.

“Government certified” and “government recognised” are specific claims, not generic marketing labels. Recognition depends on the actual awarding body, institution, programme and applicable scheme.

01

Verify the issuer

Ask which organisation issues the certificate and whether it can be checked through an official verification channel.

02

Check the authorisation

Request the institute's valid affiliation or authorisation details and verify them with the relevant official organisation.

03

Get written course terms

Confirm syllabus, eligibility, attendance, assessments, total fees, exam charges and refund rules before paying.
