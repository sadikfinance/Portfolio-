01 / Investment thesis

Start with a real problem. Prove why your solution wins.

Investors need a sharp explanation of who has the problem, how often it occurs, what current options fail to solve, and why this team can deliver a better outcome.

01 — PROBLEM

The unmet need

Describe the painful, frequent problem using customer interviews, research and specific evidence—not assumptions.

02 — SOLUTION

Your differentiated answer

Show the product or service, how it works, and the measurable improvement over current alternatives.

03 — WHY NOW

The timing advantage

Explain what changed in technology, customer behaviour, regulation or cost that makes this the right moment.

02 / Market & model

A credible business model, not just a big market number.

Build the case from a reachable customer segment outward. Use sources for market estimates and show how the company can acquire, serve and retain customers profitably.

A
Target customer

Define buyer, user, geography, segment size and the strongest early-adopter profile.

B
Revenue streams

Set out pricing, contract size, repeat purchases, subscriptions or transaction fees.

C
Go-to-market

Explain acquisition channels, sales cycle, conversion assumptions and distribution partners.

D
Unit economics

Track gross margin, acquisition cost, lifetime value, payback period and retention when available.

03 / Proof & traction

Evidence that reduces investor uncertainty.

Show only metrics you can substantiate. If the startup is pre-launch, replace traction metrics with customer discovery, prototype tests and validated milestones.

CUSTOMER SIGNAL

Interviews & pilots

Add completed interviews, pilot agreements, waitlist quality or signed letters of intent—with dates and definitions.

PRODUCT PROGRESS

What is working now

Describe the MVP, usage, repeat behaviour, product reliability and the next technical milestone.

COMMERCIAL PROOF

Revenue & retention

Publish verified revenue, active customers, churn and growth periods. Label forecasts separately from actuals.

04 / Startup agenda

A roadmap investors can hold the team accountable to.

Use time-bound milestones with one owner, a measurable outcome and a budget. Update this agenda as priorities change.

Phase 01 · Validate

Problem / customer fit

Interview target customers, test willingness to pay and define the initial market.

Phase 02 · Build

MVP / pilot

Ship the smallest useful product, run pilots and measure activation and feedback.

Phase 03 · Prove

Repeatable sales

Confirm retention, refine pricing and test repeatable acquisition channels.

Phase 04 · Scale

Expand responsibly

Hire for bottlenecks, strengthen operations and enter the next segment when ready.

05 / Funding plan

Capital tied to specific milestones.

State the amount sought, instrument, runway and the measurable milestones this round is intended to achieve. Make the use of funds add up to 100%.
