02 / Buyer journey

How sourcing works here.

The site is a request-for-quotation (RFQ) style platform, not a regular consumer shopping cart. Final pricing depends on the buyer's specifications and supplier quotations.

01 · DESCRIBE

Tell us your requirement

Product, quantity, target budget, delivery place and date.

02 · MATCH

Find possible suppliers

The future sourcing team checks product fit, MOQ and commercial terms.

03 · COMPARE

Review quotations

Compare pricing, samples, lead times, delivery and payment conditions.

04 · CONFIRM

Agree the order

Buyer and supplier confirm the contract; any EASTWARD fee is disclosed upfront.
