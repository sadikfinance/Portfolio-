01 / The startup story

Built around a simple
business problem.

Small buyers often know what they need, but not which supplier can deliver the right quality, quantity, price and timeline. EASTWARD tests whether a structured sourcing service can make that process easier.

THE PROBLEM

Too many calls. Too little clarity.

Buyers chase contacts, repeat the same requirements, and compare quotations that arrive in different formats.

THE APPROACH

A clearer sourcing workflow.

Collect a buyer brief once, contact relevant suppliers, and organise quotes with minimum order quantities and delivery terms.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Trust is the product.

Start with a narrow category, verify details carefully, and earn repeat business through transparent communication.

Example use case · not a real customer

A Kolkata café needs 500 staff T-shirts.

The owner fills out one sourcing brief instead of messaging ten different sellers.

  • Specify sizes, fabric, colour, print and required date.
  • EASTWARD contacts relevant suppliers and gathers comparable quotations.
  • The buyer reviews price, minimum quantity, sample and delivery terms.
  • If both sides agree, they transact under clear terms; EASTWARD charges an agreed sourcing fee or supplier commission.

02 / Product experience

From request to quote
in four clear steps.

The first version does not need a giant app. A polished website plus a disciplined manual workflow is enough to test whether customers will pay.

STEP 01 · REQUEST

Tell us what you need

Product, quantity, budget range, delivery location and date. One structured form.

↘
STEP 02 · SOURCE

Find relevant suppliers

Build a shortlist and check supplier identity, MOQ, terms and product fit.

↘
STEP 03 · COMPARE

Review clear quotations

Compare total price, samples, shipping, lead time and payment terms—not just unit cost.

↘
STEP 04 · COMPLETE

Agree terms directly

Buyer and supplier approve the deal. EASTWARD records the outcome and charges a disclosed fee if agreed.
