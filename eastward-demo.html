01 / The opportunity

Start with a real sourcing problem.

Small retailers and growing businesses may struggle to identify dependable suppliers, compare wholesale terms and coordinate bulk orders. EASTWARD would test whether a trusted sourcing workflow can solve that problem.

PROBLEM 01

Supplier discovery takes time

Buyers often rely on scattered contacts, referrals and repeated calls to find the right supplier.

PROBLEM 02

Quotes are hard to compare

Prices, minimum quantities, samples and delivery terms may arrive in different formats.

SOLUTION

One structured enquiry

Collect buyer requirements once, source relevant quotes and present comparable options with clear terms.

02 / Business model

How EASTWARD could work.

Begin as a service-led sourcing business, learn from real transactions, and only automate the steps customers repeatedly need.

Buyer submits request

Product, quantity, budget range, location and delivery timeline.

Team finds suppliers

Check product fit, supplier details, MOQ, quote validity and terms.

Buyer compares quotes

Present options transparently; do not imply suppliers are verified unless checks were completed.

Earn a service fee

Charge a disclosed sourcing fee or agreed commission when a suitable order is completed.

Revenue stream A

Fixed sourcing fee for a qualified requirement, agreed before work begins.

Revenue stream B

Supplier-paid commission under a clear written agreement.

Revenue stream C

Premium services such as sample coordination, repeat-order assistance or custom sourcing.

03 / Execution plan

A staged roadmap, not a big-bang launch.

Move to the next stage only when the previous stage has evidence to support it. These are proposed milestones, not completed achievements.

PHASE 01 · WEEKS 1–2
Validate the problem

Interview 20–25 target buyers; record existing methods, pain points and willingness to pay.

Proposed
PHASE 02 · WEEKS 2–4
Build a supplier shortlist

Contact suppliers in one category; compare MOQ, samples, terms and references.

Proposed
PHASE 03 · MONTH 2
Run a manual pilot

Process a small number of real enquiries using a basic website and documented workflow.

Proposed
PHASE 04 · MONTH 3
Measure conversion and service quality

Track enquiry-to-quote, quote-to-order, response time, repeat demand and contribution margin.

Proposed
PHASE 05 · MONTHS 4–6
Decide whether to automate

Build only the features that reduce proven operational friction; expand categories only with evidence.

05 / India readiness

Compliance depends on the actual business.

This checklist is a starting point for discussion with a qualified CA, CS or lawyer—not a determination that every item applies.

Before taking orders

  • Choose a suitable business structure and document founder roles.
  • Review supplier and buyer terms, refunds, liability and dispute handling.
  • Check privacy practices for customer contact and enquiry data.
  • Confirm whether GST registration and invoicing obligations apply.

Before raising capital

  • Keep accurate accounts and a defensible financial model.
  • Document ownership of code, brand and other intellectual property.
  • Check instrument, company and investor-related requirements.
  • Assess eligibility before claiming any government startup recognition.

Official starting points: Startup India · Ministry of Corporate Affairs · GST Portal
